About us

Welcome to NexaCare Health Centre

NexaCare is a health centre with its main centre in Gatua, Kitale and branches at Maili Saba, Manor House, Meru Farm, Tiwani, Eldoret and Nairobi.

We offer outpatient consultations, maternal and child health, laboratory and pharmacy services, family planning, emergency care, minor procedures and first aid, counselling, physiotherapy, homecare and telemedicine. Service availability can vary by branch, so please check with the branch you plan to visit.

Our approach

Modern Medicine, Compassionate Touch. We combine modern clinical practice with a respectful, personal approach.

Frequently asked questions

Where are NexaCare branches?

See the branches page. NexaCare has a main centre in Gatua, Kitale and branches at Maili Saba, Manor House, Meru Farm, Tiwani, Eldoret and Nairobi.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome, and you can also request an appointment.

Is homecare available?

Please see the homecare page or call us to discuss your situation.
