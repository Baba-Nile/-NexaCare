This page gives general health information in plain language. It does not diagnose, and it does not replace advice from a qualified health professional. Having a guide here does not mean NexaCare offers specialist care for every condition.

High blood pressure (hypertension)

High blood pressure means the force of blood against your artery walls is consistently higher than it should be.

Diabetes awareness

Diabetes is a long-term condition in which the body cannot properly control the level of sugar (glucose) in the blood.

Health guide · Long-term conditions

Malaria: prevention and when to get tested

Malaria is an illness spread by the bites of infected mosquitoes.

Health guide · Infectious diseases

Healthy ageing

Healthy ageing is about staying as independent, active and connected as possible as we get older.

Health guide · Older adults

Fall prevention for older adults

A fall is when a person unintentionally comes to rest on the ground or a lower level.
