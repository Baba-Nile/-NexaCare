Emergency care

Care for sudden, serious illness or injury.

About this service

Emergency care is for sudden, serious illness or injury. If you or someone else is in danger, do not wait: go to the nearest emergency facility or call emergency services.

In an emergency

Do not wait for an appointment or use a website form. Go to the nearest emergency facility or call emergency services now.

Request appointment +254 726 094 695

What to expect

  • A quick assessment to judge how urgent the problem is.
  • Immediate care to stabilise a patient where possible.
  • Advice on further treatment, which may include referral to another facility.

Who it may be for

  • People with sudden, severe symptoms
  • People with injuries that need prompt attention

How to prepare

  • Call ahead if you can.
  • Bring someone who can help give information.
  • Bring a list of medicines and any records you have.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as an emergency?

Examples include chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, seizures, sudden weakness or confusion, serious injury and pregnancy danger signs. When in doubt, seek help immediately.

Is emergency care available 24 hours?

Please confirm current emergency hours with NexaCare by phone before travelling. In a life-threatening situation, go to the nearest emergency facility.
