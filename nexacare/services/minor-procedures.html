About this service

Minor procedures and first aid cover small, short treatments and immediate care for minor injuries, such as cleaning and dressing a wound.

Request appointment +254 726 094 695

What to expect

  • Assessment of the injury or problem.
  • An explanation of what the procedure involves.
  • Wound care and dressing where appropriate.
  • Aftercare advice, including signs of infection to watch for.

Who it may be for

  • People with minor cuts, burns or wounds
  • Patients who need dressing changes as advised by a clinician

How to prepare

  • While travelling, cover a bleeding wound with a clean cloth and press firmly.
  • Bring your vaccination record if you have one.
  • Tell staff about any allergies.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a wound needs professional care?

Seek care if bleeding does not stop with firm pressure, the wound is deep or dirty, it was caused by an animal bite, or it shows signs of infection such as spreading redness, swelling, pus or fever.

Which procedures are offered?

Please call and describe the situation in general terms. The team will tell you whether it can be dealt with at the centre.

This page gives general health information in plain language. It does not diagnose, and it does not replace advice from a qualified health professional. Specific tests and treatments vary, so please call to ask about your situation.
