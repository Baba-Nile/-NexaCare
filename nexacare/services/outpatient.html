About this service

An outpatient consultation is a visit where you see a clinician, receive an assessment and go home the same day. It is often the first step for a new health concern, a follow-up or a general check-up.

Request appointment +254 726 094 695

What to expect

  • A conversation about your symptoms, health history and concerns.
  • A clinical assessment, which may include an examination.
  • Advice on next steps, which may include tests, treatment or referral where needed.
  • Guidance on when to return and which warning signs to watch for.

Who it may be for

  • Adults and children with new or ongoing health concerns
  • People who want a general check-up or a blood pressure check
  • Anyone unsure which service they need; we can point you in the right direction

How to prepare

  • Bring your medicines, or a list of them.
  • Bring earlier medical records or test results if you have them.
  • Write down your symptoms, when they started and your questions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

NexaCare welcomes walk-in patients. You can also request an appointment to help us plan your visit. A request is not confirmed until we contact you.

Can I bring a family member?

Many patients come with a relative or friend. Please tell the team if you need extra support with mobility or communication.
