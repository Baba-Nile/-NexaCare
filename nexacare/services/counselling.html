About this service

Counselling offers a private space to talk through stress, worries, relationships or difficult life events with a counsellor.

Request appointment +254 726 094 695

What to expect

  • A respectful, non-judgemental conversation.
  • Time to explain your concerns at your own pace.
  • Support to explore ways of coping.
  • Guidance on further support if it would help.

Who it may be for

  • People feeling stressed, anxious or low
  • Families dealing with illness, loss or change
  • Carers who need support

How to prepare

  • Choose a time when you can talk freely.
  • Think about what you hope to get from the session.
  • Ask how sessions work and how your privacy is protected.

Frequently asked questions

Is counselling the same as medical treatment?

No. Counselling is supportive talking. If a clinician thinks other care could help, they can discuss options with you.

What if I am in crisis?

If you may harm yourself or someone else, get help immediately: contact emergency services, go to the nearest health facility or reach out to someone you trust.
