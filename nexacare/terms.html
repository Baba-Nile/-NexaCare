The terms for using the NexaCare Health Centre website.

Information only

Content on this website is general information. It does not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a qualified health professional.

Emergencies

Do not use this website, forms or email in an emergency. Go to the nearest emergency facility or call emergency services.

Requests are not bookings

Sending a form is a request. An appointment or visit exists only when NexaCare confirms it to you.

Services and prices

Service availability and prices are confirmed by NexaCare and may change.

Links and images

External links lead to sites we do not control. Some images are illustrative and do not necessarily show NexaCare staff, patients or facilities.
