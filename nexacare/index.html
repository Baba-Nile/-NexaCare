Welcome to NexaCare

NexaCare Health Centre is a health centre in Gatua, Kitale. You can see a clinician, have tests done and collect medicines in one place, and ask about care at home or by telemedicine.

Outpatient consultations

An outpatient consultation is a visit where you see a clinician, receive an assessment and go home the same day. It is often the first step for a new health concern, a follow-up or a general check-up.
