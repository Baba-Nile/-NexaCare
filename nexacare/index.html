About NexaCare

One name, several places to get care

NexaCare Health Centre serves patients from a main centre in Gatua, Kitale and six branches. Choose the location that suits you, check its details and request an appointment in a few clicks.

Every visit starts with a respectful conversation. Not every service is available at every branch, so we confirm this with you when you book.

Appointments

Request an appointment in three steps

No account needed. Share the basics and we will take it from there.

  1. Choose your branch

    Pick the NexaCare location that is easiest for you to reach.

  2. Tell us what you need

    Share your name, telephone number and the service you are asking about.

  3. We contact you

    The team calls to confirm a date and time. Your appointment is confirmed only when we do.
