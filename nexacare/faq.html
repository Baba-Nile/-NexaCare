Frequently asked questions

Branches

Where are NexaCare branches?

NexaCare has a main centre in Gatua, Kitale and six branches. See the branches page to find yours.

How do I choose a branch?

Use the branch search on the branches page, or choose your preferred branch when you request an appointment. If you are not sure, choose “No preference” and the team will help.

Do all branches offer the same services?

Not necessarily. Service availability can vary between branches. Each branch page lists services once they are confirmed, and the team will tell you what your branch offers when you book.

Appointments

Do I need an appointment?

NexaCare welcomes walk-in patients. You can also request an appointment to plan your visit. A request is not confirmed until the team contacts you.

How will I know my appointment is confirmed?

The team will contact you by your preferred method to agree a date and time.

Services

Which services are offered?

See the services page. If you need a specific test or procedure, please call your branch and ask.

Is emergency care available 24 hours?

Please confirm current emergency hours by phone before travelling. In a life-threatening emergency, go to the nearest emergency facility or call emergency services.

Homecare

How do I ask about homecare?

Use the homecare request form or call us. A visit is only confirmed after we speak with you and check availability.

How do I ask about fees?

Please ask when we contact you, or call. Prices are confirmed by NexaCare.

Preparing for a visit

What should I bring?

Your medicines or a list of them, earlier medical records or test results, and a list of questions.

Can I bring someone with me?

Many patients do. Please tell the team if you need extra support.

Contacting us

How can I reach NexaCare?

Call the central line on +254 726 094 695 or email nexacarehc@gmail.com. See the contact page for every branch.

Is the online form for emergencies?

No. Forms are not monitored around the clock. For urgent problems go to the nearest emergency facility or call emergency services.

Billing

What do services cost?

Fees are confirmed by NexaCare. Please call or ask at your branch.

Still have a question?

Call us or send a message.
